MY FAVOURITE ARTIST

CG5

Charlie Duncan Green, popularly known as CG5, is an American songwriter, music producer and YouTuber. He is well known for creating nerdcore hip-hop music, particularly songs inspired by video games and internet culture.

About CG5

CG5 is a talented musician who has gained popularity through his creative music and entertaining content. Apart from making music, he is also involved in gaming and is the director of his horror series, Project U Move.

Some Interesting Facts

  • His real name is Charlie Duncan Green.
  • He was born on May 10, 1999.
  • He is from Phoenix, Arizona, USA.
  • He is a songwriter and music producer.
  • He creates gaming-related content.
  • The 5 in CG5 represents the fifth generation of Charlie Green.

My Favourite Songs

Here are my top five favourite songs by CG5, arranged in order of preference:

  1. My Opinions
  2. I Don't Like You
  3. Scared of You
  4. Entranced
  5. I See a Dreamer

Why I Love His Music

I enjoy CG5's music because of his creativity, unique style and ability to turn interesting ideas into entertaining songs. His music is enjoyable and different from what I usually hear.
